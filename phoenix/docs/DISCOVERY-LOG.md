# Discovery log

Everything customers and prospects tell us, in their words.

Process in [`12-FOUNDER-LED-ACQUISITION.md §4`](12-FOUNDER-LED-ACQUISITION.md).

---

## The rule

> **Capture verbatim. Categorise weekly. Never both at once.**

Categorising at the moment of capture destroys the language, and the language is
the product. *"I'm flying blind"* and *"we lack visibility into performance"*
mean the same thing; only one is a sentence a human would say out loud, and the
second is what happens when you paraphrase into a form field.

**Rule of three:** one customer saying something is a conversation. Three is a
finding.

## 3 · Requests

A request only counts if the follow-up question was asked:
**"would you pay more for that?"**

| # | Date | Company | Request *(verbatim)* | Pay more? | Prompted or unprompted |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | | |

Unprompted requests are worth several prompted ones. Nothing here gets built
during the freeze — three clients asking for the same thing makes it a candidate,
and candidates wait ([ADR 0010](adr/0010-architecture-is-frozen.md)).

## 5 · Renewal and churn

### Renewals

| Date | Company | Month # | Reason given *(verbatim)* | Price change? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | |

### Churn — exit interview, within a week, no exceptions

```markdown
### X-001 · <company> · <date> · <months as a client>

**1. When did you first think about stopping?**
> verbatim — the real churn moment is usually weeks before the notice

**2. What did you expect that didn't happen?**
> verbatim

**3. What did we do that you'd want a replacement to also do?**
> verbatim — this is the product

**4. What were you comparing us to?**
> verbatim

**5. What would we have had to do to keep you?**
> verbatim

**What I think actually happened:** (separate from what they said)
```

**A churn interview is worth more than a renewal.** A renewal says something
worked. Churn says which thing did not, and the customer is finally free to be
blunt about it.

likely way the phase fails while appearing to succeed.
