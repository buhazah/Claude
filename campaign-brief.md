## Brand DNA Summary

Dog is Human makes human-grade, vet-formulated supplements for dogs — built on the belief that the same ingredient standards health-conscious people demand for themselves should extend to their pets. The brand speaks with a science-backed but warm authority, targeting dog parents aged 28-45 who treat their dogs as family and feel let down by a pet supplement industry full of fillers, vague sourcing, and marketing fluff. Every product, including the DM-01 Daily Multivitamin soft chew, is made in Vermont with 12 research-backed ingredients — positioned not as a pet product but as a wellness product that happens to be for dogs.

## Campaign Concepts

### Concept 1: "Read the Label"
**Hypothesis:** Dog parents who already scrutinize their own supplement labels will immediately connect with the "why won't you do the same for your dog?" framing — driving high intent clicks from the most health-literate segment of the audience.
**Primary Message (PAS):** Most dog supplements are packed with fillers, artificial binders, and ingredients you can't pronounce — the same stuff you'd never put in your own body. Your dog can't read the label. You can. DM-01 is made with 12 research-backed ingredients, vet-formulated, human-grade — the standard your dog deserves.
**Secondary Message (BAB — A/B variant):** Before DM-01, your dog's daily vitamin probably had more filler than function. After switching, you'll know exactly what's going into them — every ingredient, every dose, every day. That's the Dog is Human standard.
**Tone:** Direct, a little confrontational, then reassuring. Respects the audience's intelligence. No panic — just clarity.
**Visual Direction:** Tight macro shot of the DM-01 soft chew resting on a clean cream (#F5EFE0) surface beside a human multivitamin bottle — visual parallel without text. Warm, editorial lighting. Minimal props. The chew is the hero.
**Target Platforms:** Facebook Feed (primary — longer copy reads well), Instagram Feed (secondary — visual hook does heavy lifting)
**CTA:** "See Every Ingredient →" (links to PDP ingredient transparency section)
**Addresses:** Pain point — fillers and distrust of pet supplement industry; desire for vet-trusted without a vet visit

Lifestyle photography with a clean depth of field. Color palette: warm naturals, cream tones, earthy greens. The dog is the hero; no product visible in the primary frame (product enters in carousel slide 2 or Stories second frame).

**Hypothesis:** Founder-story creative builds the trust layer that health-skeptical audiences need before converting on a $60-80/month subscription — a real origin story from two University of Chicago grads signals rigor, not just branding, and differentiates sharply from commodity pet supplement brands.
**Primary Message (PAS):** Two University of Chicago grads. Dogs they treated like family. And a pet supplement aisle that felt like a dead end — flashy packaging, mystery ingredients, no real science behind any of it. They spent two years working with veterinarians and food scientists to build something different: the DM-01 Daily Multivitamin. Human-grade. Vet-formulated. Made in Vermont. Because your dog deserves the same standard you do.
**Secondary Message (BAB — A/B variant):** Before Dog is Human existed, there was no supplement that met the ingredient standard two dog-obsessed researchers were willing to accept. After two years of vet collaboration and formulation work, DM-01 was born — 12 research-backed ingredients in a soft chew your dog will actually want to eat. This is what it looks like when the bar is set by people who care.
**Tone:** Storytelling-first, earnest, specific. Names the founders' background without being boastful. Feels like the brand has nothing to hide — because it doesn't.
**Visual Direction:** Founder-adjacent lifestyle shot — a home kitchen or clean wooden table in warm morning light, DM-01 jar open beside a notebook or printed research page (slightly blurred in background), soft chew placed in foreground on cream surface. Deep warm tones, #F5EFE0 and #8B7355 palette throughout. The image should feel like "the morning this brand was figured out." No faces required — the scene does the work.
**Target Platforms:** Facebook Feed (long-form copy reads naturally in feed), Instagram Stories (split into two frames: frame 1 = the problem, frame 2 = the solution/product)
**CTA:** "Meet the Standard →" (links to About/Mission page, then retargets to PDP)
**Addresses:** Trust deficit — health-conscious buyers who need to know who is behind the product before they subscribe
